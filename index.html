Common Motherboard Issues We Solve

Dead Board (No Power / No Fan Spin)

Caused by power surge, blown standby regulator, or shorted MOSFETs on the 12V rail.

No Display / Beep Sound Error

RAM slot failure, northbridge/southbridge communication loss, or corrupted BIOS.

USB Ports Short Circuit & Restart Issue

Damaged front/back USB data pins causing motherboard auto-shutdown or freezing.
